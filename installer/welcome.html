StreamTranslate OBS Plugin

This installs the StreamTranslate audio filter for OBS Studio - live translated captions captured directly inside OBS. No browser tab, no mic permission.

After installing:

  1. Restart OBS
  2. Right-click your mic/audio source, then: Filters > + > StreamTranslate
  3. Paste your Plugin Key from your StreamTranslate account
  4. Add your overlay as a Browser Source to show the captions

Full setup guide: streamtranslate.live/obs-plugin.html

Get your Plugin Key at streamtranslate.live
